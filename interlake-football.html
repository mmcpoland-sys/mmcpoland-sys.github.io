Interlake High School • 1991

Interlake Football, 1991

Matt McPoland quarterbacked the Interlake Saints in his senior season in 1991. He passed for 1,282 yards and nine touchdowns, was named the team MVP and received the Team Points Award. Contemporary coverage named him second-team All-Eastside quarterback.

Notable games and coverage

  • November 7, 1991 — Look, Ma, 1,000 yards! — Seattle Times feature
  • 1991 — Juanita: two touchdown passes to Rob Roach in a 21–14 Interlake victory
  • 1991 — Inglemoor: 206 passing yards and two touchdowns
