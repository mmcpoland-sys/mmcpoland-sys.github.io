Pacific Lutheran University • 1993–1996

Pacific Lutheran Baseball, 1993–1996

Matt McPoland pitched for the Pacific Lutheran University Lutes. Preserved season statistics show a 1–2 record in 1993, 3–0 with a 1.20 ERA in 1994, and 6–3 with a 3.32 ERA in 1995. The 1996 season pitching totals are not yet verified.

Notable seasons

  • 1993 — 1–2 record, 33 innings, 4.36 ERA
  • 1994 — 3–0 record, 15 innings, 1.20 ERA
  • 1995 — 6–3 record, 65 innings, 3.32 ERA; NCIC Pitcher of the Week
