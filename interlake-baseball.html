Interlake High School • 1991–1992

Interlake Baseball, 1991–1992

Matt pitched for the Interlake Saints. In 1992 he earned second-team All-Crest recognition as a pitcher and recorded five saves, leading the league according to contemporary reporting. Interlake reached the AAA state tournament.

Notable coverage

  • 1992 — Second-team All-Crest pitcher and league-leading five saves
  • 1992 — Interlake advanced to the AAA state baseball tournament
  • 1992 — Selected for the KingCo all-state feeder series as a pitcher
